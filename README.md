## 👋 About Me

I'm **Mihail** (everyone calls me **Misha**), based in **Dublin, Ireland**.

I spent 15 years leading kitchen teams, where I learned to stay calm under pressure, run tight processes, and keep a team working together. I've taken those habits into **cybersecurity** and **secure software development**.

I'm studying **Networking & Systems Security at CCT College Dublin** *(update: graduated / current year / next step)*, and I back it up with a home lab where I break things on purpose and then fix them. I build web apps with **Next.js and TypeScript**, dig through traffic in **Wireshark**, and automate the boring parts with **Python** and **Bash**.

I like understanding systems from the ground up: how a packet travels, why a config is weak, and how to close the gap.
